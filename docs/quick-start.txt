PRISM KEYBOARD STUDIO 2.5

Close the old Prism window, then open the updated desktop shortcut.
Saved looks are kept. The first normal launch backs up your profile library to
profiles.before-v2.5.json in %LOCALAPPDATA%\PrismKeyboardStudio.

QUICK START
1. Presets: choose Use preset on a card. Color palettes are above the cards.
2. Customize: adjust colors, speed, painted keys or audio.
3. Set Brightness, then Apply to keyboard.
4. Save look keeps your settings in Saved looks.

WHAT CHANGED
Less text, larger pill-shaped buttons, rounded tabs and bigger color swatches.
Hover for extra instructions and pattern descriptions. Help has the full basics.
In short windows, scroll Customize to see the lower controls. Brightness and
Apply stay visible. Presets is always the first button in the sidebar.

CONTROLS
Speed: 0-1000%, with quick 100%, 250%, 500% and 1000% buttons. The low range is
unchanged; 1000% is ten times the former maximum. Static looks and Audio do not
use animation speed. Brightness applies to every light; 0% turns them all off.

Paint: select keys, then Paint selected. Brush mode lets you paint by dragging.
Reset selected returns keys to the pattern. Clear all paint removes painted colors.
Audio: choose PC audio or a microphone, then Listen and Apply. Mono is mirrored.
Hover over colors for frequency ranges. Nothing is recorded or uploaded.

Edits apply live after Apply. Keep Prism open for animations. Pause stops updates
and audio capture. More contains Rename, Duplicate, Restore saved look, Import,
Export and Delete. Restore saved look discards changes to the saved profile.

KEYBOARDS
Close other lighting controllers first. Your full-size Huntsman V3 Pro can use
the direct USB connection. For other models, click the keyboard name, search for
the exact model, then Connect keyboard. See KEYBOARD COMPATIBILITY.html for the
152 exact USB variants and evidence; only this PC's Huntsman was hardware-tested.
Static-only models require Apply for each change and cannot stream animations.

The supplied local helper closes with Prism. No drivers, firmware, services or
startup entries are installed. Advanced can use an existing local OpenRGB server.
Keep bridge, _internal and source beside the application.

Release (in Help) restores initial brightness on direct USB or initial colors
through the bridge. It does not reconstruct an earlier animated hardware effect.
Prism controls lighting only, not actuation, macros or key assignments.
