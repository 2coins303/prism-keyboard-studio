PRISM 2.5 - PRESETS AND LIGHT PATTERNS

Open Presets at the top left. This is also the first screen when Prism starts.
The four Color presets sit above the 31 pattern preview cards. Search or choose
a category, then click Use preset. Customize opens automatically. Set brightness
and click Apply to keyboard; Save look keeps your settings under Saved looks.

Presets normally use their suggested colors. Turn on Keep my colors to retain yours. In Customize, click any of the three color swatches
to edit it. Rainbow effects use the full spectrum; Audio uses its six frequency
colors. Painted keys stay on top of the selected pattern.

Brightness remains at the bottom of both screens. Set 0-100%; 0% turns
off every LED, including audio and painted colors. Speed adjusts motion from 0-1000%. The old 0-100% range is unchanged;
1000% is ten times the old maximum. Use the percentage entry or quick buttons
in Customize > Colors and motion. Save look remembers it.
 Direction
is available for patterns that use it; Left/Up reverse radial rotation or rings.
Rain starts with Down selected. Ripple is automatic, not triggered by keystrokes.

Keep Prism open for animations. Static-only keyboards offer the four Static
patterns and painted colors with manual Apply. Without a physical map, lighting
is uniform; Adjust layout enables spatial motion. Zone-based keyboards show less
detail than per-key lighting.

AUDIO
  Audio: Bass through treble respond to your selected audio source.

GLOW AND AMBIENCE
  Breathing: Your palette slowly fades in and out.
  Rainbow cycle (new): The whole keyboard smoothly cycles through the spectrum.
  Aurora (new): Soft curtains of color drift above a dark background.
  Ocean waves (new): Layered waves wash across the keys like a moonlit sea.
  Fire (new): Warm, flickering plumes rise from the bottom row.
  Lava lamp (new): Glowing blobs slowly float and merge.
  Heartbeat (new): A gentle double pulse runs through your palette.
  Sunset drift (new): A warm horizon slowly moves through your colors.
  Plasma (new): Intersecting color waves form a moving, liquid texture.
  Galaxy (new): Rotating arms of color glow around a bright core.

SPARKLES AND PARTICLES
  Sparkle: Bright individual lights over a dim palette.
  Rain (new): Independent trails of light fall across the keyboard.
  Starfield (new): Scattered stars brighten and fade against the dark.
  Twinkle (new): A dense, gentle shimmer of independent lights.
  Fireflies (new): Small glowing lights wander softly over the keys.
  Confetti (new): Colored flecks gently appear and dissolve.

STATIC
  Gradient: A smooth blend across the keyboard.
  Solid: One clean color on every light.
  Checkerboard (new): Alternating blocks of your first two palette colors.
  Diagonal bands (new): Diagonal ribbons blend all three of your colors.

WAVES AND MOTION
  Rainbow wave: A moving ribbon of the full color spectrum.
  Color flow: Soft palette colors roll across the keys.
  Rainbow spiral (new): A rainbow curls around the center of your keyboard.
  Candy stripes (new): Bands of your three colors slide across the keyboard.
  Color chase (new): Three bright runners chase each other across the keys.
  Comet (new): A bright head sweeps by with a long, fading tail.
  Scanner (new): A soft beam sweeps out and returns.
  Ripple (new): Automatic rings expand from the center in a repeating loop.
  Radar (new): A rotating beam sweeps around a dim circular field.
  Pinwheel (new): Three soft palette blades turn around the center.
