# Changelog

## 2.5.1

- Fixed color-picker buttons inheriting the selected swatch color and losing their contrast and padding.
- OK, Cancel, Pick Screen Color, and Add to Custom Colors remain readable for every swatch color.
- Added a regression check for palette, brush, and audio color pickers at normal and enlarged display scaling.

## 2.5.0

- Shorter text across Presets, Customize, Paint, Audio, setup, and Help.
- Larger pill-shaped buttons, rounded tabs, and larger color swatches.
- Pattern descriptions and detailed guidance available on hover.
- Audio controls use the main page's scrolling layout.
- Existing profiles, lighting output, brightness, and animation timing preserved.

## 2.4.0

- Speed range extended to 1000%, with a maximum ten times the former maximum.
- Original 0–100% speeds preserved.
- Exact percentage entry and quick 100%, 250%, 500%, and 1000% buttons.
- Speed changes preserve the animation's current position.

## 2.3.1

- Fixed clipped button text when resizing the window.

## 2.3.0

- Presets-first navigation, visible color shortcuts, and simpler saved-look controls.

## 2.2.0

- Brightness slider and numeric entry available throughout the app.

## 2.1.0

- Expanded to 31 lighting patterns with a searchable visual gallery.

## 2.0.0

- Bundled local OpenRGB bridge and an exact-variant compatibility catalog.

## 1.0.0

- Direct Huntsman V3 Pro lighting, per-key painting, saved looks, and stereo audio.
